Haotian Tan is a Ph.D. candidate in Information Science at the Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST), where he is a member of the Human AI Interaction Laboratory under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Sakriani Sakti.

His research focuses on simultaneous speech translation and real-time spoken language systems. In particular, he studies low-latency translation policies and system architectures for continuous speech, together with related topics in automatic speech recognition, speech synthesis, and speech language models.

Research Interests

Simultaneous Speech Translation

Low-latency translation of continuous and unsegmented speech, with an emphasis on quality–latency trade-offs and READ/WRITE decision policies.

Automatic Speech Recognition

Robust speech understanding and streaming ASR components for multilingual speech translation systems.

Speech Synthesis

Incremental TTS and speech-to-speech translation for natural real-time cross-lingual communication.

Speech & Language Models

Efficient integration of large language and speech models into practical real-time spoken interaction systems.

Participation: April 2024 – March 2026

A Study on Multi-modal Automatic Simultaneous Interpretation System and Evaluation Method

JSPS KAKENHI · Grant-in-Aid for Scientific Research (S) · 21H05054

Within this project, Haotian led the design and implementation of an LLM-driven real-time speech-to-speech simultaneous interpretation prototype. His work integrated streaming speech translation, READ/WRITE decision policies, and incremental speech synthesis, with a focus on balancing translation quality and latency. He delivered a working research prototype for experimental evaluation and system demonstrations.
